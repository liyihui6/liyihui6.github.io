Yihui Li 李艺晖

Ph.D. Student
South China Normal University

  • ●Guangzhou, China
  • ⓄORCID

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at South China Normal University in Guangzhou, China, since September 2024. I received my master’s degree from the School of Computer and Information Engineering, Jiangxi Agricultural University, in 2023 through recommendation-based admission. My research focuses on machine learning and data mining, particularly feature selection, partial-label learning, and multi-label learning, with interdisciplinary applications in healthcare (such as dementia risk prediction) and brain–computer interfaces (fMRI decoding).
