I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at South China Normal University in Guangzhou, China, since September 2024. I received my M.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Jiangxi Agricultural University in 2023 and my B.Eng. degree in Network Engineering in 2020. My research focuses on machine learning and data mining, particularly feature selection, partial-label learning, multi-label learning, and label distribution learning, with cross-disciplinary applications to healthcare such as dementia risk prediction.
